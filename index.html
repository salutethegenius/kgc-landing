The Foundation Beneath Everything We Build

Verity Suite
Sovereign AI Infrastructure for the Caribbean
VerityOS™ + Hummingbird
VerityOS is the local AI operating system. Hummingbird (VerityLite) is the hardware it runs on. Together, they are the foundation of a Bahamian-owned digital infrastructure layer - built on Bahamian soil, governed by Bahamian principles, designed to last.

Every other product KGC builds will eventually run on this substrate. Every byte of Bahamian institutional data we hold is being prepared to live here, not on foreign cloud infrastructure.
Currently in pilot. General availability 2027.

What we do

Independent Digital Infrastructure,
Built Locally

KGC develops practical, locally managed digital systems that help The Bahamas responsibly manage data, expand economic participation, and prepare for the AI era. We are not replacing institutions. We are strengthening the digital tools they rely on.

🧠
Local AI Systems

We build and train AI platforms grounded in Bahamian context. VerityOS runs locally and is designed for schools, businesses, and public sector use.

VerityOS · VerityLite

Resilient, Offline First Technology
