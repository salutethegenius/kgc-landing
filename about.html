What we do

Strengthening the tools
institutions rely on

KGC develops practical, locally managed digital systems that help The Bahamas responsibly manage data, expand economic participation, and prepare for the AI era. We are not replacing institutions. We are strengthening the digital tools they rely on.

🧠
Local AI Systems

We build and train AI platforms grounded in Bahamian context. VerityOS runs locally and is designed for schools, businesses, and public sector use. When intelligence systems reflect local realities, they become more accurate, more useful, and more trustworthy.

The Founder's Desk
“I am not trying to recreate
Silicon Valley. I am building
Bahamian excellence.”

I grew up here. I understand both the talent and the gaps. After living and working abroad, I became convinced that the Bahamas does not lack ability. We need stronger digital systems, deeper knowledge, and coordinated opportunity.

Artificial intelligence is not foreign. It is a tool. And if we participate in shaping it, we ensure it reflects our values, our laws, and our realities.

This work is not about control. It is about contribution. It is about building bridges so the next generation does not have to figure everything out alone.

Kenneth C Moncur
Founder & Group Managing Director, Kemis Group of Companies

The Vision - A Bahamas where:
  • 01 Government spending is transparent and searchable - Citizens can easily understand how public funds are allocated. BahamasOpenData makes this accessible.
  • 02 Students access world class digital education from anywhere in the country - Kemis Academy prepares Bahamians for careers in AI and technology, without requiring relocation.
  • 03 Entrepreneurs participate in global commerce - KemisPay expands digital payment access for freelancers and small businesses.
  • 04 Legal information is understandable - LawBey helps citizens explore Bahamian statutes and legal frameworks in plain language.
  • 05 Local developers build local systems - Talent stays, grows, and leads.
The model

Build locally. Scale responsibly. Held permanently. Each KGC brand contributes to a connected ecosystem powered by The Verity Suite, our locally managed AI foundation. The goal is long-term national capacity, sustainable revenue, and technology that reflects the community it serves - across generations, not investor cycles.
